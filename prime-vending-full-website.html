About Prime Vending

Prime Vending places customer-operated machines in businesses across the United States. We supply the equipment, install it, keep it stocked and serviced, and pay the location a share of the revenue every month.

Our aim is simple: business owners should not have to buy equipment, manage staff or chase paperwork to earn extra income from space they already have.

Common questions

Quick answers before you apply.

Does it cost anything?

No. We supply, install and service the machine at no charge to you.

What kind of machines do you place?

Prime Vending machines are customer-operated entertainment and vending machines. We explain the exact model and how it works during your application, before you agree to anything.

How and when do I get paid?

Your revenue share is paid once a month, with a statement for that machine.

What if the machine breaks?

Call or message us. We handle repairs and replacements.

Is it allowed where I am?

Rules differ by state and city, so we review your area before any install and tell you what applies.

How long does installation take?

Timing depends on your location and local requirements. We confirm a date after reviewing your application.

What does the agreement look like?

We go through the terms with you in plain language before you sign. Nothing is binding until you agree.
